## 3. Defects found and fixed in this pass

See `bug-audit/BUG_TRACKER.md` §Independent re-audit (DEF-036..DEF-042).
Summary: VIN sub-volt truncation, daemon discovery silence (wrong serial /
blocked devices), `--dump-config --menu` silent no-op, `--dashboard-text`
ignoring `[monitor] output`, undefined 0xC0 multirail byte reported as a rail
mode, wrong `--watch` name in the `--dashboard-text --menu` conflict, and the
energy integral freezing above a 300 s cadence (plus the daemon's wall-clock
inconsistency it exposed).

Each fix ships with a regression test; the collected total grew 889 → 898.

Evidence: ruff/format/mypy green locally and in CI; the first batch is green in
ci#41 (`71fd135`, 896 tests) and the final batch in ci#43 (`047e2d3`, run
`40458242818`, 898 tests) including coverage, smoke, accelerated soak, .deb and
the frozen-release job.

## 4. Residue (honest gaps)

- No real PSU: every device protocol claim, the OCP protection write, fan/Zero
  Fan/buzzer writes, per-model E1h and VIN are simulated-only.
- arm64 binaries are built by CI but never executed here.
- No real root/systemd/udev install, no desktop-browser pass, no 24 h/72 h soak.
- `--show-log` still cannot distinguish a NAK from an empty black box (NR-004).
- `--watch` does not reopen a device that disappears mid-run (documented as a
  daemon-only recovery, left unchanged).
